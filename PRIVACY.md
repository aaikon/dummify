# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-15. Applies to the dummify browser extension for Chrome and Firefox._

dummify collects no data.

- **No data collection.** dummify doesn't collect, store, or transmit any personal information, browsing history,
  page content, or usage statistics. There is no telemetry, analytics, or crash reporting.
- **No network requests.** dummify never contacts a server, including ours. Its rules ship inside the extension
  and only change when the extension itself is updated through the browser's add-on store.
- **What stays in your browser.** Your settings (which kinds of content to hide, and the sites where dummify is
  off) are saved with the browser's extension storage. If you use your browser's sync, the browser may sync them
  between your own devices; dummify itself never sends them anywhere. The number of hidden items on each tab is
  kept in memory only while that tab is open.
- **Why it needs access to all sites.** dummify hides parts of the pages you visit (AI answers, short-form video,
  algorithmic suggestions). To do that, it reads each page's structure in your browser and hides the matching
  parts. Nothing it reads leaves your device.
- **No third parties.** dummify shares nothing with anyone and has no ads.

Uninstalling dummify removes its settings.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here and in the store listings, with a new date.
