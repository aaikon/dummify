## Long description

dummify takes the "smart" out of the web: no AI answers, no autoplay rabbit holes, no Shorts.

It quietly hides the features that try to think for you:

- **AI features:** AI Overviews and AI Mode in Google, "Ask AI" and "Summarize with AI" buttons, AI chat
  widgets on help and docs sites.
- **Short-form video:** YouTube Shorts, Instagram and Facebook Reels. TikTok is blocked entirely.
- **Automated suggestions:** recommendation feeds and autoplay, "People also ask" and search autocomplete,
  smart replies and smart compose.

One switch turns it on or off, everywhere or just for the site you're on. The toolbar shows how much was
hidden on the current page.

**Private by design.** dummify makes no network requests and collects no data. Your settings stay in your
browser.
